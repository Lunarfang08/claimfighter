  Denial Code: CO-11 - Diagnosis inconsistent with procedure
  
  Explanation: The diagnosis code submitted (S83.511A - ACL sprain, initial encounter) does not support the medical necessity for a knee x-ray. X-rays are not the appropriate imaging modality for soft tissue injuries like ligament sprains. MRI would be the standard of care for ACL evaluation.

  - Degenerative joint disease/osteoarthritis

  ACL injuries require MRI for diagnosis, not x-ray.
